Meta Platform Data Compliance

User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 2026

InstaAutoDM respects your privacy and complies with Meta Platform terms regarding user data deletion. According to the Facebook Platform rules, we provide the following instructions for users who wish to remove their activities and data associated with InstaAutoDM.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you have connected your Instagram / Facebook account to InstaAutoDM and want your account information and message logs deleted, you can do so through any of the following methods:

Method 1: Instant Disconnect via Dashboard

Log in to your InstaAutoDM account, navigate to Settings → Connected Accounts, and click Disconnect Account. This immediately purges your stored access tokens, Instagram account details, and cached messages from our active database.

Method 2: Remove App via Facebook Settings
  1. Go to your Facebook Profile's Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  2. Look for Apps and Websites where you will see all the apps linked to your account.
  3. Search for InstaAutoDM.
  4. Click the Remove button.
  5. Check the box to request that Facebook notify us to delete your data.
Method 3: Direct Email Request

Send an email to support@instaautodm.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and your connected Instagram handle. Our team will delete all associated records within 48 hours and send a confirmation.

Confirmation Code

Upon receiving a data deletion request, your confirmation code will be issued as DEL-CONFIRM-XXXXX for verification purposes.
