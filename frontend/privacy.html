Airvix 🔒 Meta Platform Compliant

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2026 • Version 3.3

Airvix Technologies ("Airvix", "we", "our", or "us") provides conversation automation and engagement workflows for Instagram Professional and Creator accounts via official Meta Graph APIs. This Privacy Policy details how we access, collect, process, store, and protect your information when you access our platform or connect your Instagram account.

1. Account & User Information Collected

When you register for an Airvix workspace, we collect the following basic information:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, business email address, and encrypted password hash (salted using bcrypt).
  • Subscription Details: Selected pricing tier (Free, Pro, Agency, Enterprise), billing period start date, and automated reply quota consumption.
  • Workspace Configuration: Custom automation rules, keyword triggers, response templates, and notification preferences.

2. Instagram & Meta Information Accessed

When you connect your Instagram account via official Meta OAuth 2.0 authorization, Airvix accesses strictly the data necessary to provide automated responses:

  • Profile Metadata: Instagram User ID, handle (@username), profile picture URL, account type (Professional/Business/Creator), and follower count.
  • Connected Facebook Page: Facebook Page ID and Page name linked to your Instagram Professional account (used solely to discover and route webhook events).
  • Media Metadata: Recent Reels and Post identifiers, captions, media type, and timestamp (to allow targeting automations to specific posts).
  • Inbound Public Comments: Comment ID, comment text, commenter Instagram scoped ID and username, and timestamp.
  • Inbound Direct Messages: Message ID, conversation thread ID, incoming text payload, sender scoped ID, and message timestamp.

3. Scope of Processed Comments & Messages

Airvix operates on a strict minimization principle. We process only:

  • Inbound comments and direct messages that match creator-configured keyword triggers or active automated follow-up sequences.
  • Interactions occurring within Meta's allowable interaction windows: maximum 7 days for comment private replies, and within 24 hours of user initiation for direct messages.
  • We never access or read personal friend feeds, private personal chats unrelated to your professional inbox, or third-party browsing data.

4. Stored Identifiers & Data Minimization

To deliver reliable automation, idempotency, and audit logs, Airvix stores only minimal technical identifiers:

  • Meta Instagram Scoped User IDs (IGSID) and Page-Scoped IDs (PSID).
  • Unique Comment IDs and Meta Message IDs.
  • Deterministic idempotency keys (e.g. comm_{commentId}, msg_{messageId}) to mathematically guarantee duplicate replies are never dispatched during network re-transmissions.

5. Purpose of Data Collection

The information collected is used exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • Delivering creator-configured automated replies to customer comments and inquiries.
  • Fulfilling automated lead magnet delivery (e.g., sending resource links requested by commenters).
  • Displaying your creator inbox and conversation reply histories within your private workspace.
  • Enforcing fair use, rate limits, and platform compliance policies.

6. Operational Usage & Rate Limiting

All automated messages are routed through an asynchronous queue with natural jittered pacing (5–30 seconds) and per-account concurrency caps (maximum 2 concurrent sends, 30 requests/min). This prevents traffic bursts, respects Meta rate limits, and protects creator account standing.

7. Data Retention & Purge Schedule

  • Active Connections: Data is retained for the duration of your active workspace subscription.
  • Disconnected Accounts: When an account is disconnected, stored access tokens are immediately purged.
  • Data Deletion Requests: When requested via in-app deletion or Meta's Data Deletion Callback, all associated tokens, conversation history, and automation rules are permanently deleted from database storage within 24 to 48 hours.

8. Storage Location & Cryptographic Security

Military-Grade Encryption at Rest & In Transit: All Meta OAuth access tokens and sensitive secrets are stored using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with unique per-record initialization vectors and authentication tags. Database storage is hosted in Amazon Web Services (AWS us-east-1) via managed PostgreSQL with mandatory TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.

9. Access Isolation & Third-Party Disclosure

  • Tenant Isolation: Every database query is strictly filtered by workspace tenant ID (user_id). One workspace can never access another creator's conversations or rules.
  • Zero Third-Party Sale: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer user data, comments, or direct message contents to advertisers, brokers, or external AI model trainers.
  • Personnel Access: Only authorized Airvix security engineers may access system infrastructure under strict least-privilege protocols for debugging and compliance.

10. How to Disconnect Your Account

You have full autonomy over your Instagram connection. You can disconnect at any time:

  1. Log in to your Airvix workspace dashboard.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Connected Accounts.
  3. Click Disconnect Account. All stored tokens are instantly deleted and webhooks cease processing.

11. User Data Deletion & GDPR / CCPA Rights

In accordance with GDPR, CCPA, and Meta Platform Terms, you have the absolute right to erasure. You can request deletion through:

  • Meta App Settings: Removing Airvix from your Facebook/Instagram connected apps automatically triggers our real-time Data Deletion Callback.
  • Account Erasure: Click "Delete My Account" in Creator Settings to initiate an immediate cascade purge of all user records.
  • Direct Request: Email our Data Protection Officer at support@airvix.com.

You can check the audit status of any deletion request anytime at /data-deletion-status using your confirmation code.
