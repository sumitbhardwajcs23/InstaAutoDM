Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

ReplyOS ("we", "our", or "us") provides conversation automation tools for Instagram professional and business accounts in accordance with Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

1. Information We Access

When you connect your Instagram account, we access only the data necessary to provide automated replies:

  • Instagram User ID and Page ID
  • Inbound comments and direct messages sent to your account
  • Timestamps to enforce Meta's 24-hour messaging window and 7-day comment age cutoff

2. How Information is Used

  • Triggering automated responses based on creator-configured keyword rules
  • Preventing spam and enforcing messaging rate limits
  • Displaying analytics and usage statistics in your dashboard

3. Security & Cryptography

All Meta access tokens and webhook secrets are stored using AES-256-GCM encryption. We do not sell, rent, or transfer user message data to third parties.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Message logs are retained solely for idempotency and audit logs. Users may request deletion of their data at any time via our automated deletion callback or by disconnecting their account in Settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact support at privacy@replyos.com.
